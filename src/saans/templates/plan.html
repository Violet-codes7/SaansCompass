Protect yourself every day

  • {% if plan.profile and plan.profile.has_n95_mask %}Wear your N95 outdoors on Poor or worse days. It only works if it seals: pinch the nose clip and check for gaps.{% else %}A well-fitted N95 or FFP2 mask filters most fine particles; cloth and surgical masks do little.{% endif %}
  • Keep windows shut at the worst hours and air rooms out at the cleanest one.
  • Indoors, avoid incense, mosquito coils, frying smoke and sweeping dry dust; wet-mop instead. A HEPA air purifier sized for the room helps most in the bedroom.
  • Drink water and keep your usual medicines with you.

Get help now if

You have severe breathlessness, chest pain or pressure, blue lips, confusion or fainting. Call 112. This page is general advice, not a diagnosis.
